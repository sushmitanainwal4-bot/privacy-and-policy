Speak Zeno — Account & Data Deletion

App: Speak Zeno (com.speakzeno.app) • Developer: Sushmita Nainwal • Contact: sushmitanainwal4@gmail.com

Speak Zeno allows you to permanently delete your anonymous account and all associated personal records at any time — either directly inside the Android/Web app or by submitting a deletion request on this page.

Option 1: Delete Immediately Inside the Speak Zeno App

  1. Open the Speak Zeno app on your phone or browser.
  2. Tap the Settings icon (top-right gear or Home quick menu).
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Tap Confirm Delete to permanently erase your profile and sign out.

Option 2: Request Account & Data Deletion Online

If you have already uninstalled Speak Zeno or cannot sign in, submit your account details below. Verified web and email deletion requests are processed within 7 days.

What Data Is Deleted vs. Never Collected

  • Deleted Permanently: Your anonymous profile/alias, date of birth, selected talk vibe & topics, region, recent caller connections, call history logs, block lists, and Web Push notification subscriptions.
  • Never Collected or Recorded: Speak Zeno does not collect phone numbers or payment cards and does not record live voice call audio.
  • Retention Period: In-app deletions take effect immediately. Web/email deletion requests are completed and purged from Cloud Firestore within 7 days, except where limited safety/abuse moderation records must be retained to enforce bans.
