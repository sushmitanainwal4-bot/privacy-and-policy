1. Overview & Purpose

Welcome to Speak Zeno ("the App", "we", "us", or "our"). Speak Zeno is a free, mobile-friendly peer-to-peer English speaking practice platform designed to connect language learners worldwide for live, one-on-one 15-minute voice conversations.

Your privacy and trust are our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how it is used, and how we safeguard your personal privacy while using Speak Zeno.

2. Information We Collect

We practice data minimization. We only collect the minimal information necessary to deliver practice matchmaking and maintain community safety:

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Display Name: The friendly name you choose to identify yourself to practice partners.
  • Date of Birth: Used solely to verify age compliance (13+) and prevent minors from being matched with unauthorized groups.
  • Language Profile (Optional): Your native language, estimated English level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced), selected conversation topics/interests, and country of residence.
Zero Phone Number & Zero Financial Data Policy Speak Zeno does never ask for, collect, store, or require your phone number, credit card, bank account, or government identification. All communication is conducted purely through encrypted WebRTC internet data streams.

B. Automatically Collected Information

  • Session Status & Telemetry: Online presence, active matchmaking status, call duration counters (to enforce the 15-minute equal session limit), and timestamp of your last active session.
  • Technical Information: Basic device identifiers, browser type, and operating system used by our infrastructure for connection diagnostic and anti-abuse purposes.

3. Microphone Access & Voice Calling

Speak Zeno requests access to your device's Microphone strictly during active voice calls with speaking partners.

  • No Audio Recording: We do not record, transcribe, tap, or store your voice audio on any server.
  • Real-Time Peer Streaming: Voice audio is streamed directly in real time between callers via the Agora WebRTC Real-Time Network. Once the call terminates, no audio data persists.
  • Full User Control: You have complete real-time control to mute or unmute your microphone and disconnect the call at any moment.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

To deliver a reliable and free service, Speak Zeno integrates the following trusted industry-standard service providers:

  • Agora RTC (Real-Time Communication): Powers real-time voice streaming between matched language partners without exposing personal contact details.
    Agora Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase (Firestore & Hosting): Manages cloud database persistence for matchmaking queues, user accounts, and community safety records.
    Google Privacy & Terms
  • Google AdMob: Serves sponsored advertisements to keep the app 100% free of charge for learners worldwide.

5. Google AdMob & Advertising Disclosures

Speak Zeno uses Google AdMob (Publisher ID: ca-app-pub-1348353671193682) to display non-intrusive banner ads and post-session interstitial ads after completed speaking calls.

Google AdMob may collect and process information such as:

  • Device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID or IDFA)
  • Approximate geographic location (country or city level)
  • Ad interaction metrics and diagnostics to prevent fraud

Google uses this information in accordance with its privacy practices. You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services by visiting:
How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.

You may opt out of personalized ads at any time by modifying your device's Google/Android ad settings or visiting Google Ads Settings.

6. Safety, Moderation & Reporting

To preserve a polite, safe, and productive educational atmosphere:

  • Users can report or block any partner immediately during or after a call.
  • Reported users are reviewed by moderators and prohibited from future matches with reporting parties.
  • Accounts engaging in abusive behavior, harassment, vulgarity, or unsolicited promotions are permanently suspended.

7. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA / GDPR)

Speak Zeno is intended for users who are at least 13 years of age (or 16 years of age in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If we discover that a user under the legal age has provided personal information without verified parental consent, we will promptly delete the account and associated records.

8. Data Retention, Security & Deletion Rights

We retain your profile data only for as long as your account remains active.

How to Delete Your Data You have the complete right to request the permanent deletion of your profile and data at any time. You can do this directly within the app:
Navigate to Settings → Danger Zone → Remove / Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile and all associated data from our database.
