1. Overview & Purpose

Welcome to Speak Zeno ("the App", "we", "us", or "our", package ID com.speakzeno.app). Speak Zeno is a free anonymous 1-on-1 voice talk platform designed to connect people worldwide for live, private 15-minute audio conversations without revealing phone numbers or real identities.

Your privacy, anonymity, and trust are our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy explains what minimal information we collect, how it is used, how notifications and ads work, and how you can permanently delete your account and data at any time.

2. Information We Collect

We practice strict data minimization and collect only what is needed for anonymous voice matchmaking, notifications, and community safety:

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Anonymous Alias / Display Name: The nickname you choose so other callers can talk with you without seeing your real name.
  • Date of Birth: Used solely to verify age eligibility (13+) and allow you to sign back into your alias profile.
  • Anonymous Persona (Optional): Your chosen talk vibe/style (e.g., Chill Listener, Casual Chat, Open Talk, Deep & Thoughtful, Debate & Ideas), preferred language, conversation topics, and general country or region.
Zero Phone Number & Zero Financial Data Policy Speak Zeno does not collect, store, or require your phone number, government ID, credit card, or banking information. All voice conversations occur over encrypted real-time internet audio channels.

B. Automatically Collected Information

  • Session Status & Call Records: Online availability status, matchmaking state, call duration counters (enforcing the 15-minute session timer), recent caller history, and block/report records for safety.
  • Technical & Diagnostic Data: Basic browser/OS metadata and connection diagnostics used to maintain real-time audio reliability and prevent abuse.

3. Microphone Access & Voice Calling

Speak Zeno requests permission to use your device Microphone strictly while you are in an active voice call or running a microphone self-test in Settings.

  • No Voice Recording: We do not record, store, or transcribe your voice audio on our servers.
  • Real-Time Audio Streaming: Audio is transmitted live between callers via Agora RTC. Once a call ends, audio streams cease immediately.
  • Full User Control: You can mute your microphone or hang up at any instant.

4. Push & Online Arrival Notifications

With your explicit permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS on Android 13+ or browser notification permission), Speak Zeno may store an anonymous Web Push subscription endpoint or schedule local device alerts so your phone notifies you when another caller comes online or sends a call request.

  • You can customize or disable online arrival alerts, caller scope (Anyone vs. Previous Callers), and sounds anytime under Settings → Notifications.
  • Deleting your account removes your associated push subscription records.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

Speak Zeno uses trusted service providers strictly to power voice calling, cloud persistence, and free access:

  • Agora RTC (Real-Time Communication): Delivers low-latency anonymous voice streaming without exposing phone numbers.
    Agora Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore & Authentication): Stores anonymous profiles, matchmaking queues, recent caller lists, and safety reports/blocks.
    Google Privacy & Terms
  • Google AdMob: Serves non-intrusive ads so Speak Zeno remains 100% free.

6. Google AdMob & Advertising Disclosures

Speak Zeno uses Google AdMob (App ID: ca-app-pub-1348353671193682~1176674196) to display banner ads and post-call interstitial ads.

Google AdMob may collect or process:

  • Device advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / AAID)
  • Approximate location (country/region level)
  • Ad engagement metrics and fraud-prevention diagnostics

Learn how Google uses data at Google Partner Sites Policy, or manage personalized ads in Google Ads Settings.

7. Safety, Moderation & Reporting

  • Callers can report or block any user immediately during or after a call.
  • Blocked users cannot match with or call you again.
  • Accounts violating community rules (harassment, hate speech, abuse, or solicitation) are suspended permanently.

8. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA / GDPR)

Speak Zeno is intended only for users aged 13 years or older (or 16+ where required by local law such as in the EEA/UK). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If an underage account is discovered or reported, it is deleted promptly.

9. Data Retention, Security & Deletion Rights

We retain your anonymous profile, call history, and safety preferences only while your account remains active. You can permanently delete your account and associated data at any time:

How to Permanently Delete Your Account & Data Option 1 (Inside the App — Instant): Open Speak Zeno → Settings → tap Delete Account → tap Confirm Delete.

Option 2 (Web Portal / Google Play Data Deletion Link): Visit our dedicated Account & Data Deletion Page to submit a deletion request online or by email. Requests submitted via web or email are verified and completed within 7 days.
